Marina

An anime character who sits on my Mac desktop and talks back.

Source on GitHub · built on riko_project

She lives in a transparent window that stays on top of everything else. You talk to her or type, and she answers out loud, lip syncs, and acts out anything the model writes in asterisks, like *rolls her eyes*, instead of reading it aloud. Her voice and her hearing both run on your own machine.

Download Marina for macOS

A ready-to-run app, 664 MB. Open the dmg, drag Marina to Applications, done — no build step and nothing to install. Apple Silicon, macOS 13 or newer. It is signed ad-hoc rather than notarized, so the first time you open it run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Marina.app.

Try her here first

The same renderer the app uses, running in your browser. Here she only has a few pre-recorded lines, because the real app needs a language model and runs the voice on your own machine. Type something, hover over her, or leave the page alone for a bit and she'll talk first.
