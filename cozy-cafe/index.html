Recipe book

How to make a drink

  1. Take a cup in the size they asked for (cups).
  2. Espresso: grind beans first, then put the cup under the machine. It pours on its own, so go do something else and come back. Leave it too long and it turns bitter.
  3. Hot milk: start the steamer, then pour into the cup when it's ready. Pour quickly for latte art. One pitcher does two drinks.
  4. Iced drinks: ice and cold milk come from the fridge.
  5. Add syrup and toppings last, then click the customer.
  6. Need both hands? Set a cup down on the front counter.
